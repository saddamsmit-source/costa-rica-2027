Facilities
 

The Hotel Fleur the Lys intimate and elegant restaurant Obelisco is well known and respected for its New Costa Rican Cuisine. In addition to our superb dinner menu, we serve a complimentary breakfast to each of our guests.

Our cosmopolitan bar is a great place for sharing special romantic moments, as well as meeting Costa Ricans and international travelers. Every Thursday and Saturday, our guests enjoy a "Happy Hour" from 5:30 p.m. until 6:30 p.m. On Friday nights, we have live music from 6:30 p.m. until 8:30 p.m
