Frequently Asked Questions

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in or late check-out may be available upon request.

Do you offer airport transfers?

We can help arrange airport transfers from Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) or La Fortuna airstrip. Please contact us in advance to coordinate.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before your check-in date. Cancellations within 48 hours may be subject to a one-night charge.

Are pets allowed?

We love animals, but unfortunately we are unable to accommodate pets at the lodge to preserve the comfort of all guests and the local wildlife.

What tours and activities are nearby?

La Fortuna offers volcano hikes, hot springs, waterfall tours, zip-lining, rafting, and wildlife walks. We're happy to help you plan and book activities.
