Swiss quality with warm hospitality
 

Fleur de Lys is a small, enchanting, discrete Hotel located in the cultural district of San Jos, Costa Rica. This newly restored Victorian mansion offers 31 individually decorated rooms, to make you feel at home. Rather than impersonal numbers, every room is lovingly named after a species of flower native to Costa Rica. Native artwork adorns the walls of each room, lobbies and corridors of the Hotel. Warm woods and soft colors provide an atmosphere for complete relaxation, and large comfortable beds guarantee a good night's sleep. Discover the hotel's charming atrium gardens with lush tropical planting.

Our front desk (open 24/7) clerks will gladly assist you in reconfirming flights or tours, as well as providing information on additional points of interest within Costa Rica. Visit Travel Costa Rica to learn what the country has to offer for tourism.
