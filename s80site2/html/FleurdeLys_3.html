Rooms

Fleur de Lys consists of 30 rooms in 4 categories all individually furnished and decorated with native artwork. All the rooms have cable/TV, telephone, radio clock, ceiling fan, private bath with bidet faucet, hot water and hair dryer. No two rooms look alike.

SMOKE FREE HOTEL.

 


STANDARD

 

The Standard Rooms are located in the historical (declared heritage of Costa Rica) and in the newly built part on the ground floor. They will delight you with their decoration and views on the various green internal patios (typical in the old Costa Rican homes). All rooms offer television, wireless internet, telephone, fan and are individually decorated. The bathrooms are fully equipped with shower and hairdryer.


 

STANDARD SUPERIOR

 

These rooms are located on the second and third floor in the historical and new part of the hotel.
They are perfect for travelers looking for spacious and enchanting accommodation. Some have a balcony or a private patio and all are furnished with television, wireless internet, telephone, and fan. The bathrooms are fully equipped with shower and hairdryer.


 

SUITES

 

Our Suites are situated on the second and third floor of the charming historical part of the hotel. They correspond to families with up to two children and the more demanding travelers appreciate the additional space and location of these rooms. The suites are equipped with television, wireless internet, telephone, minibar and fan. The bathrooms are fully equipped with shower and hairdryer.

 

MASTER SUITES WITH JACUZZI

 

They are located in the new part of our hotel and offer plenty of space in a comfortable environment. Most suitable for honeymooners, corporate executives or those planning on staying more than a couple of nights. All rooms are fitted with a king size bed and sofa (sofa bed), television, desk, wireless internet, telephone, minibar, safe, fan and of course a private Jacuzzi. The spacious bathrooms are fully equipped with shower and hairdryer.
