Location
 

Hotel Fleur de Lys is located in the cultural heart of San Jos, the nations capital. Situated in a quiet street close to numerous restaurants, theaters, casinos and shops, Fleur de Lys is Gateways to many other attractions in town. Within minutes walk of the hotel lobby, youll discover the National Museum, the Gold Museum, the National Theater, the pre-Columbian Jade Museum, the 1920s Metal School, built by G. Eiffel-builder of the Eiffel Tower as well as a variety of interesting and beautiful city parks and much more.

We are located on 13th street, between 2 and 6th avenue, one block from the National Museum.
