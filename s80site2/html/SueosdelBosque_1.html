ABOUT SUEÑOS DEL BOSQUE LODGE

We are a family business located in San Gerardo de Dota, where we offer lodging and food service, as well as tours to different places and attractions of the place. The Sueños del Bosque Lodge in San Gerardo de Dota has been in existence for 20 years, as its first steps were given under the tourist focus of camping areas.

ROOMS

We currently have 10 rustic rooms surrounded by plants and birds, to convert what was a useless farm, today in one of the most visited hotels in the area.
